A study coach that runs entirely on your phone

Tell Pathfinder what you want to achieve. It finds your skill gaps, lays out a learning pathway and writes a weekly study plan that changes as you progress. A Llama model does the reasoning on the phone itself, so it works offline and your data never leaves the device.

What it does

Five jobs from the brief, one feature each

Llama handles the language: understanding goals, explaining choices, writing plans. Plain code handles scores and schedules, because a small on-device model is unreliable at arithmetic and long structured plans.

How it works

From a goal to a plan in five steps

  1. Describe your goal

    Chat in your own words. The assistant asks up to three follow-up questions.

  2. Take a short quiz

    8 to 12 questions show which skills are strong and where the gaps are.

  3. Get your pathway

    An ordered list of skills, each with a reason and free resources.

  4. Follow your plan

    Daily sessions sized to your hours. Tick them off and rate them.

  5. Check in weekly

    A mini-quiz and a reflection update your plan for the next week.

Everything runs on the phone

App screensKotlin and Jetpack Compose: onboarding chat, quiz, pathway, this week, check-in
Learning engineScores skill gaps, orders the pathway by prerequisites, schedules hours, updates mastery
LLM wrapperBuilds prompts in the Llama 3 chat format, checks JSON replies, streams text to the screens
Local databaseProfile, skill levels, plans and progress
Bundled contentSkill graphs, quiz banks, resources
ExecuTorch runtimeLlama 3.2 1B Instruct, 4-bit weights, XNNPACK backend on the Arm CPU

The model is converted once on a computer into an ExecuTorch .pte file and copied to the phone. After that the app needs no network connection.

Technology

What we chose and why

Model
Llama 3.2 1B Instruct, quantised to 4-bit weights. 3B is the upgrade if the phone is fast enough.
Runtime
ExecuTorch v1.5.1 with the XNNPACK backend, which runs on any Arm Android phone's CPU.
App
Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, using ExecuTorch's Android library (executorch-android 1.5.1).
Privacy
On-device only. Learner data stays in a local database and the model needs no network.
